# Witch visual source

"Witch" by Quaternius, downloaded from [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/QBEOV9ZUT8), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The original GLB was converted to FBX for the Unity scene. Its six flat colors are reproduced as Unity materials. The imported Idle animation drives the visual; scene movement still comes from WitchBossAI.
